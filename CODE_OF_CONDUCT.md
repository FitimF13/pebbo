# Code of conduct

Pebbo follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good intent, welcome newcomers, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment, insults and publishing other people's private information are not tolerated.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially.
